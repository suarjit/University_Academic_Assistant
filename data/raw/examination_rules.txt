
# GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY
## Examination Rules – Academic Year 2026–2027

### 1. Examination System
Greenfield University follows a semester-based examination system.

- Each academic year consists of two semesters.
- Odd semester examinations are normally held in November and December.
- Even semester examinations are normally held in April and May.

### 2. Internal Assessment
- Internal assessment carries 30% of the total marks.
- End-semester examinations carry 70% of the total marks.
- Internal assessment may include assignments, class tests, presentations and attendance.

### 3. Minimum Passing Marks
- Students must obtain at least 40% marks in each course to pass.
- Students must separately pass the internal assessment and end-semester examination.
- Students who fail a course must appear for the applicable supplementary examination.

### 4. Attendance Requirements
- Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course.
- Students with attendance below 75% may be prevented from appearing in the end-semester examination.
- Medical leave may be considered after submission of valid supporting documents.

### 5. Examination Conduct
- Students must carry their university identity cards to the examination hall.
- Mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are prohibited.
- Any form of cheating or examination malpractice may result in disciplinary action.

### 6. Examination Duration
- Standard theory examinations are three hours long.
- Practical examination duration depends on the course requirements.

### 7. Results and Revaluation
- Examination results are published through the student portal.
- Students may apply for revaluation within 15 days of result publication.
- Revaluation applications require payment of the prescribed fee.

### 8. Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail a course may register for the supplementary examination according to the university examination schedule.
