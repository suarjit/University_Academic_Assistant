
# GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY
## Campus Facilities – Academic Year 2026–2027

### 1. Central Library
- The central library is open Monday to Saturday.
- Opening hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Students can borrow up to four books at a time.
- The standard borrowing period is 14 days.
- Students must return books before the due date to avoid late fines.

### 2. Computer Laboratories
- The university has five computer laboratories.
- Laboratories are equipped with internet-connected computers.
- Lab operating hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Students must use their university login credentials to access lab computers.

### 3. Hostel Facilities
- Separate hostel accommodation is available for male and female students.
- Hostel rooms are subject to availability.
- Hostel residents must follow university accommodation rules.
- Hostel applications are submitted through the Student Affairs Office.

### 4. Cafeteria
- The campus cafeteria operates Monday to Saturday.
- Opening hours: 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- The cafeteria provides breakfast, lunch, snacks and beverages.

### 5. Sports Facilities
- The university provides facilities for cricket, football, basketball and badminton.
- Sports facilities are available from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Students must register with the Sports Office to use selected facilities.

### 6. Medical Centre
- A campus medical centre provides basic first aid and health assistance.
- Operating hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Emergency assistance is coordinated through the Student Affairs Office.

### 7. Wi-Fi and Internet
- Campus Wi-Fi is available in academic buildings, the library and selected common areas.
- Students must use their university credentials to connect.
