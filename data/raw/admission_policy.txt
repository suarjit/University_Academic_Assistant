GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY

Admission Policy – Academic Year 2026–2027

1. General Information

Greenfield University is a fictional educational institution established for academic project development and testing.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Science, Arts, Commerce, Computer Science and Management.

2. Undergraduate Admission Eligibility

- Applicants must have completed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.
- Applicants must have obtained a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
- Applicants applying for reserved-category seats must meet the applicable eligibility criteria.
- Some programmes may have additional subject-specific requirements.

3. Postgraduate Admission Eligibility

- Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
- A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required.
- Programme-specific entrance examinations may apply.

4. Application Process

1. Visit the university admission portal.
2. Register using a valid email address.
3. Complete the online application form.
4. Upload the required documents.
5. Pay the application fee.
6. Submit the application before the deadline.

5. Required Documents

- Class 10 marksheet and certificate.
- Class 12 marksheet and certificate.
- Graduation marksheet for postgraduate applicants.
- Transfer certificate.
- Passport-size photograph.
- Government-issued identity proof.
- Category certificate, if applicable.

6. Application Fee

The undergraduate application fee is ₹500.
The postgraduate application fee is ₹750.
Application fees are non-refundable.

7. Admission Selection

Admission is based on academic qualifications and programme-specific selection requirements. Some programmes may require an entrance examination or interview.

8. Contact Information

Admission Office: Greenfield University
Email: admissions@greenfield.example
Office Hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

9. Important Notice

Admission deadlines and programme requirements may change. Applicants should consult the latest admission notification before applying.
