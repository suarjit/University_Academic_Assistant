
# GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY
## Scholarship Policy – Academic Year 2026–2027

### 1. Overview
Greenfield University offers merit-based and need-based scholarships to eligible students to support their education.

### 2. Merit-Based Scholarships
Students may receive the following tuition fee waivers based on their qualifying academic results:

- Students scoring 90% or above may receive a 50% tuition fee waiver.
- Students scoring between 80% and 89.99% may receive a 25% tuition fee waiver.
- Scholarship awards are subject to verification of academic records.

### 3. Need-Based Scholarships
- Need-based scholarships are available to students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds.
- Applicants must submit valid family income certificates and supporting financial documents.
- Applications are reviewed by the university scholarship committee.

### 4. Eligibility Requirements
- Students must be enrolled in a full-time university programme.
- Students must maintain satisfactory academic performance.
- Students must not have any major disciplinary violations.
- Students must submit all required scholarship documents before the announced deadline.

### 5. Scholarship Renewal
- Scholarships are reviewed at the end of each academic year.
- Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.0 for renewal.
- Students who fail to meet renewal requirements may lose their scholarship.

### 6. Application Process
Students must submit the scholarship application form with the required academic and financial documents to the Scholarship Office.
