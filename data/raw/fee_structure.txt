
# GREENFIELD UNIVERSITY
## Fee Structure – Academic Year 2026–2027

### 1. Undergraduate Programme Fees
The annual tuition fees for undergraduate programmes are:

- B.Sc.: ₹40,000 per year.
- B.A.: ₹30,000 per year.
- B.Com.: ₹35,000 per year.
- B.Tech.: ₹80,000 per year.
- BBA: ₹50,000 per year.

### 2. Postgraduate Programme Fees
The annual tuition fees for postgraduate programmes are:

- M.Sc.: ₹45,000 per year.
- M.A.: ₹35,000 per year.
- M.Com.: ₹40,000 per year.
- MBA: ₹90,000 per year.

### 3. Additional Charges
- Admission fee: ₹2,000 (one-time).
- Registration fee: ₹1,000 per year.
- Library fee: ₹1,500 per year.
- Laboratory fee: ₹3,000 per year for programmes requiring laboratory facilities.
- Examination fee: ₹1,000 per semester.

### 4. Payment Schedule
- Tuition fees must be paid at the beginning of each academic year.
- Students may pay tuition fees in two equal installments.
- The first installment is due before the start of the academic year.
- The second installment is due at the beginning of the second semester.

### 5. Late Payment
A late payment fine of ₹500 is charged if fees are not paid within 15 days after the due date.

### 6. Refund Policy
- Admission fees are non-refundable.
- Tuition fee refunds are considered according to the university's withdrawal policy.
- Students must submit a written refund request to the Accounts Office.
